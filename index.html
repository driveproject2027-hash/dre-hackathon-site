FROM THE GRID TO THE ENTERPRISE
WHY DRE?

The Energy Challenge Is No Longer Just About Generation.

Enterprises increasingly face challenges related to grid instability, thermal overload, fluctuating tariffs, carbon emissions and unreliable energy access.

01 GRID INSTABILITY

Centralized distribution networks suffer from thermal overloads, localized voltage fluctuations, and vulnerability to transmission failure. Heavy commercial rooftop feed-ins exacerbate reverse power spikes during sunny afternoons.

02 ENERGY COST & RELIABILITY

Fluctuating commercial tariffs drive operational expenses high during peak hours. High inductive machine inrush currents trip fragile rural solar microgrids and accelerate battery bank degradation, straining MSME cash flow.

03 LAST-MILE ENERGY ACCESS

Hilly topography and dense forest cover make central grid extension economically prohibitive. Isolated agro-processing machinery in tribal valleys experiences weeks of downtime when an inverter trips due to lack of local diagnostic tools.

GROUND REALITY

WHY THIS MATTERS IN ANDHRA PRADESH

Here is where the challenge becomes real. Andhra Pradesh holds 9.1% of India's renewable energy potential, but localized ground realities throw specific operational bottlenecks that demand engineered solutions.

    OFFICIAL FRAMEWORK

    Rules & Guidelines

    Official competition boundaries, participation standards, and submission guidelines.

    01

    Immediate Start & Registration Deadline (5th October)

    The hackathon begins from the moment teams register. So, the sooner your team registers, the more time you will have to work on the problem statements. The last day to register for the hackathon is on 5th October.

    02

    Problem Statements & Prototype / MVP Scope

    Participating teams are to find a solution to any one of the problems. Participants should submit prototypes or a minimum viable product (MVP) of their solution. The official problem statements can be found here: Download Problem Statements (PDF) ↗.

    03

    Phase 1: Prototype Submission & PoC Link (Deadline: 9th October)

    In Phase 1 of the hackathon, teams will submit their presentation and/or a video demonstrating their solution. The last day to submit your prototype is on 9th October.
    IMPORTANT: Please make sure that you keep a copy of the PoC submission link which you will see in the confirmation notice on completing your team registration.

    04

    Originality, Equipment & Consumables

    The solutions must be the own work of participating teams. Participants should have their own equipment and consumables to help them develop a solution.

    05

    Valedictory & Certificates of Participation

    ONLY participating teams that are present for the valedictory will be awarded certificates of participation.

    06

    Cash Prizes & Implementation Grants

    Cash prizes are awarded to the top winning solutions: 1st Prize: ₹10,000 INR (10k), 2nd Prize: ₹8,000 INR (8k), 3rd Prize: ₹6,000 INR (6k), and 3 Consolation Prizes: ₹4,000 INR each (4k × 3 = ₹12,000). Total cash purse: ₹36,000 INR. All participants will also receive official Participation Certificates.

    07

    Mandatory LinkedIn Demo Submission

    All teams are required to post a working video demonstration of their project on LinkedIn. Your LinkedIn profile must be set to Public, and you must explicitly tag Laya Green Ventures in your post for your submission to be evaluated.
