### 🎨 Art Direction & Visual Hierarchy System

1. **Restrained Editorial Color Palette**:
   - **Background (Type A)**: Warm Ivory (`#F5F3ED`)
   - **Contrast Background (Type B)**: Deep Forest (`#0E3B2F`)
   - **Data Background (Type C)**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
   - **Primary Typography**: Deep Charcoal (`#17211D`)
   - **Secondary Typography**: Muted Gray-Green (`#5E6964`)
   - **Primary Brand Color**: Forest Green (`#14634D`)
   - **Muted Green**: Sage (`#71877D`)
   - **Accent**: Warm Ochre (`#C99A32`, used sparingly for figures)
   - **Borders**: Thin Warm Gray (`#D9D6CD`)

2. **Visual Rhythm (Three Section Types)**:
   - **TYPE A — EDITORIAL**: Cream ivory background, confident typography, minimal borders (Hero, Why DRE, Rules, FAQ).
   - **TYPE B — FIELD**: Deep forest-green background, cartographic mapping, high-contrast statistics, cream typography (Andhra Pradesh Context, Final Decision CTA).
   - **TYPE C — DATA**: White background, structured consulting tables, challenge cards, registration forms (Challenge Board Chapters, Bottleneck Matrix, Registration Sheet).

3. **Core Editorial Components**:
   - **Navigation**: Clean, understated sticky header where the forest-green `REGISTER NOW` button is the only strongly colored element.
   - **Hero Section**: Begins ~60px below navigation. First viewport immediately presents the event context, campaign line (*"An Inevitable Next Step."*), direct CTAs, an authentic documentary photograph of a real rural agro-processing unit with rooftop solar, and an editorial data strip (*10 real-world challenges | 4 enterprise contexts | 1 shared objective*).
   - **Editorial Transition**: Subtle horizontal divider (*FROM THE GRID TO THE ENTERPRISE*) setting publication rhythm.
   - **Why DRE?**: 3-column publication spread (*01 Grid Instability*, *02 Energy Cost & Reliability*, *03 Last-Mile Energy Access*) sitting directly on ivory with hairline dividers.
   - **Ground Reality in Andhra Pradesh**: Printed editorial cartographic map identifying *Gajuwaka*, *Atchutapuram*, *Paderu*, and *Chintapalli* with small green markers, paired with the 4 verified document statistics (**55.3 GW**, **9.1%**, **167 GW**, **<6%**).
   - **The Challenge Board (4 Visual Chapters)**:
     - *01 Agriculture & Horticulture* (PS01, PS02, PS03)
